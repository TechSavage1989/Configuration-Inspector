# Config Inspector 5.44.0

## Diagram layout

- **Details with the device, not beside it:** each switch, firewall or router shows its name above its faceplate and its model and address below it, instead of a label card to the right. Drawings are narrower, devices sit side by side more often, and pages print larger. The text sits between the links that enter the device; when the name does not fit above, it goes below and the model and address go above. A device widens only when neither fits.
- **Wireless connections pages:** access points are drawn as in a hand-made wireless page. Those on top-row ports form a row above the switch with their name, port and speed, and address over the icon; those on bottom-row ports form a row below with the details under the icon. Each link lands on its access point's icon.
