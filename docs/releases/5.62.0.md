# Config Inspector 5.62.0

## Chassis exactly as NetworkDiagramStudio draws it

- **Same panel, same jack size:** a 5400R zl2 (or 5400zl) chassis is now drawn from NetworkDiagramStudio's own chassis panel: its management module, optional management bay, module cards filling their slots and jacks at full size. Before, modules were drawn on their own, which made their ports about half the size.
- **Room for links without changing the panel:** the gap between the two module columns, or the right edge of the chassis, widens just enough for the link lanes.
- **Links clearly apart:** a link from an inner module takes its step on the side of its port row with more room (above the ports on these modules), so neighbouring steps are always at least 6 px apart instead of bunching just below the ports.
- **Port maps:** module ports are numbered as on the module (1-8), not with the slot letter.

## WAN circuits within the stack

- **Circuits in rows above the firewall:** provider circuits that do not fit side by side stack in rows within the firewall's width, as in NetworkDiagramStudio.
- **Links stay in the column:** circuit links avoid running out past the sides of the firewall and the devices below it.
