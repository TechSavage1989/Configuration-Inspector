# Config Inspector 5.30.0

## Network diagrams

- New **Network diagram** page (sidebar, once imported data contains links) that draws diagrams from the imported neighbor and monitoring data and the open configurations:
  - **Overview:** the whole site as a topology, titled after the site, with firewalls, switches and controllers by tier, links colored by type and labeled with their ports, and a legend.
  - **One page per switch stack or building:** the upstream device at the top, then the switches drawn as faceplates with their management address, name and model, links colored by type (10 Gbps+ fiber or uplink, 1 Gbps copper, access point copper) with port labels, access points and named devices cabled into their switch, the VLANs on the right and a legend. Small buildings fit on one page; a large core shows its direct neighbors and continues on the next pages.
- Edit the diagram title, then download:
  - **Visio (.vsdx):** every page as a Visio page, with editable shapes, lines and text.
  - **SVG:** the page shown.
  - **Printable HTML:** all pages, ready to print or save as PDF.
