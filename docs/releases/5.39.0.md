# Config Inspector 5.39.0

## Diagram editing that keeps up with you

- **Selection box:** while editing, drag on empty space to select every device and access point inside the box (Shift or Ctrl adds to the selection). The middle mouse button still moves the view.
- **Links snap and merge:** drag any stretch or corner of a link. A stretch dragged onto the line of another stretch of the same link snaps to it and the two merge into one straight run. **Ctrl+click** a link to add a point, or a corner to remove it; the right-click menu still adds and removes bends.
- **Link ends move with their ring:** dragging a link's end on a stencil now moves the ring on the port with it, while you drag and after.
- **Thin links are easy to grab:** when zoomed out, pressing within a few pixels of a link takes the link.
- **The view stays put:** an edit, undo or redo no longer resets the zoom or scrolls back to the start.
- **Hops everywhere:** links that cross now hop even when they belong to the same bundle of uplinks.

## Stencil port points

- **Port points editor:** under **Stencils**, **Port points** on a device's row (or double-clicking a stencil device while editing) shows the stencil large with a point for every port. Select points by click, Shift-click or a box, drag them or nudge them with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps), and line them up with **Same height**, **Same column**, **Space across** and **Space down**. **Reset** puts points back where the stencil places them. Saved points apply to every device drawn with that shape, on the diagram and the port map.

## Fixes

- **Fix:** the device search under **Stencils** (and the device list on the diagram) did nothing: rows that did not match stayed visible.
- **Fix:** messages appeared behind an open dialog, blurred and unreadable. They now show above it.
- **Fix:** bitmaps inside vendor stencil pictures follow their raster operation, so a transparent mask pair no longer covers the picture; a picture that uses an operation the app cannot draw is drawn as a faceplate instead.
- **Theme button:** the light and dark theme is now one **Dark** / **Light** button at the top of the window instead of a drop-down.
