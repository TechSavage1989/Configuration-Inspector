# Config Inspector 5.40.0

## Links avoid crossing

- A link crosses another only when no route avoids it. After the first routing pass, every line that still crosses is routed again with all the others in place, where any route without a crossing wins over one with fewer bends.

## Drawn like the reference site drawing

- Link colors follow the reference drawing: red for 10 Gbps fiber and uplinks, yellow for 1 Gbps copper and provider handoffs, green for access point copper, with thinner lines.
- The connection types sit in a column at the left of the diagram, set off by a thin rule.
- Provider handoffs and other devices are pale boxes with a dark accent bar, their name in bold and their port and address below; grouped access points are a green summary box ("1/1/41-48 | 8 APs").
- **Port map:** each port on a stencil is a numbered square in its role color, sized to the port (outlined when the link is down), instead of a dot.

## Hardware packs

- **Save hardware pack** under **Stencils** saves the vendor shapes the site's devices use, with their port points and shape choices, to one JSON file. **Import hardware pack** on another computer brings the shapes back (listed as "(hardware pack)" files), with their port points and any device choices not already made there. Visio downloads keep using the vendor shapes.

## Port points editor

- Choose a port from the **Port** list, or type its position **Across** and **Down** in percent.
- **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z) step through port changes in the editor.
- Ctrl+arrow keys nudge the selected points by 0.1 px (arrows 1 px, Shift 10 px).

## Fixes

- The browser's own right-click menu (Back, Reload, Print) no longer opens by accident; text fields keep it for copy and paste.
- Double-clicking near a thin link (zoomed out) opens its ports, as a press near it already picks it up.
