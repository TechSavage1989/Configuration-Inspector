# Config Inspector 5.60.1

## Diagrams with HA firewalls and monitoring exports

- **Switches below an HA firewall pair:** "Device and below" from a firewall now shows every device cabled to it from the level below. Before, when a core switch was cabled to both firewalls of an HA pair, it appeared under only one of them, and the other firewall's diagram showed nothing below it.
- **No made-up ports from monitoring exports:** neighbours that a monitoring export (such as Auvik) reports on VLAN interfaces, loopbacks or tunnels are routing neighbours, not cables. They are no longer drawn as links to a "VLAN10" port on each switch, which tangled the drawing.
- **Firewall HA heartbeat ports:** a switch port reported against both a firewall's data port and its HA heartbeat or management port is drawn to the data port only.
