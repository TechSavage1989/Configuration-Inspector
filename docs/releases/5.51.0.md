# Config Inspector 5.51.0

## Network diagram

- **WAN circuits head the drawing:** no link passes above a provider circuit when any other route exists. A handoff on a bottom-row port now goes around the side of its firewall up to the circuit instead of looping over the top of the circuits.
- Links to WAN circuits may cross other links (with a hop) rather than take a long detour; the rule against avoidable crossings still holds for every other link.
