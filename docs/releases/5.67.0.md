# Config Inspector 5.67.0

## Cleaner links

- **No overlapping chassis leaders:** two links leaving the same chassis module (one by the top edge, one by the bottom, or from ports a pixel or two apart) could step out at the same height and run on top of each other. Every step out of a module now has its own height.
- **No pointless dips:** a link no longer drops a short way and comes back up (or steps sideways and back) while carrying on in the same direction. This was most visible with a stack placed beside the firewall. Such a step stays only when it keeps the link from crossing another.
- **Links to an unknown port:** when the data does not say which port a link uses, the link now ends on a dashed "?" jack on the device's edge instead of seeming to go to nothing.

## Port maps on one sheet

- **Larger switches:** stack members sit closer together (slimmer member bars, smaller gaps), so the switches are drawn larger on the sheet.
- **Details fill the free space:** when the Trunks / LAGs and Port descriptions lists do not fit under the switches, they go in the column under the VLAN key instead of onto a second sheet. An eight-member stack now fits on one sheet with its switches at full width.
