# Config Inspector 5.38.0

## Network diagrams from configurations alone

- **Without monitoring data:** the Network diagram opens with only configurations loaded.
- **Links from the configurations:** links come from a port described with another device's name and from a default gateway that is another device's address. The port toward the other device is the described port, a subinterface's parent, or the trunk carrying the VLAN.
- **WAN circuits:** each firewall WAN interface is drawn as a provider circuit with its address and gateway. **Show → WAN circuits** hides them.

## Fix what the files don't show

- **Add link** draws a cable between two devices, on the ports you choose.
- **Add device** adds a device with no configuration or imported data (a provider router, an unmanaged switch).
- **Links** lists every link and where it comes from (imported data, the configurations, or by hand); remove the wrong ones, and bring them back later.
- These are kept on this computer for the site and appear on every diagram of it.

## More editing

- **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** (or **Undo** and **Redo**) step through every change, including added links and devices.
- **Port points:** drag the end of a link to move its point on a port, for stencils whose marks miss their ports.
- **Bends:** right-click a link to add or remove a bend, choose its ports, or reset its route.
- **Hops:** every crossing now hops, as in Visio.

## Stencils

- **Picker:** choose a stencil file and then a shape in it, or search every file at once; suggestions come first.
- **Several devices:** tick them to give them one shape, the automatic choice or drawn faceplates.
- **Port maps:** they use the same stencils as the diagram.
- **Artwork:** more of a stencil's picture is drawn (bitmap copies and blends). Artwork that still can't be drawn in full (gradients, EMF+ only pictures) is not used automatically, so a device is drawn cleanly instead of with a broken picture.
