# Config Inspector 5.35.0

## Links land on the right stencil ports

- **Known layouts on stencils:** for devices with a known front-panel layout, each group of ports is matched to the stencil's own ports in order, so a link on a FortiGate-120G's X3 lands on the X3 cage. SFP cages marked at both edges, and cages set lower than the RJ45 jacks, are read correctly.
- **Fix:** the diagram itself now uses the verified front-panel layouts (5.34.0 used them only on the documentation pages).
- **Newer stencils:** a shape whose product number and description name a newer model than its shape name (the 120G shape in Fortinet's 2026 stencil is named "FG-120/121F") is matched by its product details.
- **FortiGate 120G:** ports X1-X4 sit between ports 1-16 and ports 17-24, as on the device.
- **Access points** use their face-on view; side and edge views are no longer chosen automatically.

## Wireless connections drawn like the switch

- The **Wireless connections** pages now draw each switch with its access points wired to their own ports: odd ports above, even ports below. The table of access points follows.

## Group access points

- **Show → Access points → Group into one box per switch** condenses a switch's access points into one box listing their ports and model; the ports stay marked on the faceplate.

## Choose the pages

- **Choose pages** (next to the page tabs) turns the port map, wireless connections, VLANs and routing, and trunk VLAN pages on or off, and chooses which devices appear on the port map.
