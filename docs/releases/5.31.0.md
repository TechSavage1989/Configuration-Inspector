# Config Inspector 5.31.0

## Network diagrams for any part of the network

- The **Network diagram** page opens on the device of the selected configuration and everything below it, with the device above it for context.
- Choose what to draw:
  - **Device and below**, **Device connections** or **Whole site**.
  - Any device, the number of levels below it, and **Faceplates** or **Boxes**.
  - Show or hide the upstream device, access points and other devices.
  - **Choose devices** leaves out single devices.
- Click a device in the diagram to draw from it down; the path above the diagram goes back up.
- Scroll to zoom and drag to move, as on the site map, with zoom buttons and **Fit**. A new diagram is fitted to the window.

## Clearer drawings

- Switches are faceplates sized from the device's own interfaces or a built-in model catalog (Cisco Catalyst 2960/3560/3650/3750/3850/9200/9300/9500, Cisco 5508, Aruba 6100 and 2930F, HPE 2530, Meraki MS and SonicWall TZ600). Every port is numbered (odd ports on top) and stack members are drawn side by side. Uplink (SFP) ports are drawn as dark cages.
- Links reach a top-row port from above and a bottom-row port from below. They go around devices and never run on top of each other, and they hop over the links they cross. Links at 100 Mbps or slower are dashed. A port reported with several neighbors becomes a trunk with a junction dot.
- Access points and named devices sit above the switch for top-row ports and below it for bottom-row ports, with their port, name and address.
- Port names sit in bands above and below each device, so labels no longer overlap lines or each other.
- The page header shows the site and device, the model and address, and a legend.

## Downloads

- **Visio (.vsdx)** now saves the diagram shown. Each device is one group of plain shapes, so it moves as a unit.
- **Site book (.vsdx)** saves the whole site plus one page for each switch with switches or access points below it.
- **Device library (.vsdx)** saves every faceplate in the catalog to copy into your own diagrams or drag into a stencil in Visio.
- **SVG** and **Printable HTML** save the diagram shown.

## Navigation

- Every page in the sidebar has its own icon: for example a building for Site overview, a map for Site map, a diagram sheet for Network diagram and a database for Supplementary data.
