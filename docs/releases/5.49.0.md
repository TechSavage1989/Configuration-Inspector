# Config Inspector 5.49.0

## Layout

- **WAN at the top:** the diagram runs from the WAN down. Provider circuits always sit above the firewall or switch they connect to, even on a bottom-row port, whose link leaves the port downward and goes around the device to the circuit (as in the reference drawings).
- Access points and other connected devices stay within the left and right edges of the switch, firewall or stack they connect to, whenever they fit. The gap between them shrinks (down to one grid step) before a row may run past the device; a row that still cannot fit is centered on the device instead of running off one side.
- On wireless connections pages, each access point box is only as wide as its name (the icon sits above it), so a full row of access points fits within a 48-port switch.

## Default view

- By default the diagram shows the selected configuration's device and the devices below it that also have an imported configuration, plus WAN and ISP circuits. Access points, other devices, the upstream device and switches without a configuration are left out until **Show** includes them (new **Switches without a configuration** choice). A switch without a configuration that lies on the way to one that has is still drawn.

## Connection types

- A link type's legend line and its links share one style: changing the color, name, thickness or line (solid or dashed) from the legend or from a link changes both. The **Link type** dialog gains **Thickness** and **Line**.
