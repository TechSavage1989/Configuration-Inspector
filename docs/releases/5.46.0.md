# Config Inspector 5.46.0

## Straighter links

- Links use right angles and as few bends as possible: an access point's link now takes at most two bends, and the small sideways steps beside ports and access points are gone. Across the owner's site, the wireless pages went from 42 bends to 26, and small steps from 29 to 4.
- Access point boxes now sit centered on the routing grid, and those near a switch's edge may overhang it slightly so they can stay square over their port. Bends cost the router more than before, so it prefers straight runs.

## Connection types legend

- Pointing at a legend entry shows that it can be edited: it turns the accent color, is underlined, and a tooltip explains what you can do.
- Right-click an entry for its options: **Change color and name…**, **Highlight links of this type** (every other link fades until you choose **Show every link again**), **Reset to the default color and name** when it has been changed, and, for a type given to links by hand, **Return its links to their own types**.
