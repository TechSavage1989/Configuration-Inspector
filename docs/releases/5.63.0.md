# Config Inspector 5.63.0

## Arrange the diagram your way

- **Size:** in edit mode, **−** and **+** make the selected devices 10% smaller or larger, or every device when nothing is selected; the percentage between them puts them back to 100%. Ports, modules, stacks and chassis scale with the drawing and the links follow. A device shrinks only as far as its linked ports stay clearly apart.
- **Place beside:** select one or more devices or access points, choose **Left of**, **Right of**, **Above** or **Below**, then click the device to place them beside. They are centred on it with room for their links, step past anything already on that side, and when the page edge is in the way everything else moves over instead. Esc cancels. Right-clicking a device offers the same.
- **Pages:** **Move to page…** puts the selected devices on a page of their own, listed after the main diagram in the page tabs and in the Visio, SVG and printable downloads. Each page can be edited. A link between two pages ends at an off-page reference on both pages, naming the device and port at the other end and the page to turn to.

## Easier selecting and moving

- **Shift- or Ctrl-click** adds items to the selection; click a selected item again (without moving) to take it out. Dragging any selected item moves them all.
- **Shift while dragging** now keeps the move to the direction the drag started in, from its starting point (across or up and down), with a guide showing the line it keeps to.
- **Links are easy to pick:** the link a click would take lights up as the pointer comes within a few pixels of it, and a click there selects it.
- The edit bar updates as soon as a click selects something, so its buttons are ready straight away.
