# Config Inspector 5.61.0

## Device drawings shared with NetworkDiagramStudio

- **The same front panels in both apps:** switches, stack members, chassis modules and firewalls are now drawn with NetworkDiagramStudio's library of researched front panels. It covers hundreds of models, such as Aruba 2930F, 6100 and 5400R zl2 modules, Catalyst, FortiGate, SonicWall and Juniper. Each is drawn at its real proportions, with RJ45 jacks, SFP and QSFP cages, LED panels and vendor marks.
- **Links on the real jacks:** links land on the jacks where they are on the device. A port in use is ringed in its link color, or shows its optic or cable module in the cage.
- **Port maps:** each jack carries a numbered square in its port role color, on the same front panel as the diagram.
- **Stacks and chassis:** each stack member is drawn as its own switch panel, with its number and model in the top margin, clear of the lines. Chassis modules are drawn in their slots, and the 5400R management module shows its LEDs and OOBM and console ports.
- **Still editable in Visio:** the panels are plain shapes, as before. Vendor stencils you import still take the place of the drawn panel.
- A model the shared library does not cover port for port keeps the previous drawing.
