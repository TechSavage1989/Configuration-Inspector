# Config Inspector 5.66.0

## Compact, clean layouts

- **Smallest square:** with Fit to drawing (the default), the devices are arranged in the smallest square that holds them, and the choice also weighs crossings, bends and link length. Across the test network designs the drawings take about 10% less area and have about a third fewer crossings than in 5.65.0.
- **Tight stacks:** each gap between stack members is only as tall as the links in it need, instead of every gap being as tall as the busiest one.
- **Side entry for stacks:** a link to a stack member whose route passes beside the stack now enters through the gap at that member's height. It no longer loops under or over the stack and back up or down its side lane. With a stack placed beside its core, such links go from four bends to two.

## Tidy links

- **Tidy links** on the edit bar routes every link again the cleanest way the app can find. It tries several routing orders and keeps the drawing with the fewest crossings, then the fewest bends and the shortest links, in neat parallel tracks. Links you drew by hand are redrawn; **Undo** puts them back.

## Labels follow moved links

- When you move a link by hand, its port label moves with it: beside the stretch where the line now leaves the device, or alongside the line when it leaves sideways.

## Less repetition in the documentation

- **VLANs and routing** lists each address once: VLAN interface addresses only in the VLANs table, and routed ports with their addresses only in the port inventory. The page keeps loopbacks, tunnels and management addresses under **Other routed interfaces**, with a note saying where the rest are.
