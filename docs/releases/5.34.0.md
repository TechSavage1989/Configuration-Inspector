# Config Inspector 5.34.0

## Faceplates that match the hardware

- **Real port positions:** network diagram faceplates now use the same verified front-panel layouts as **Interfaces at a glance**, including a model you choose there. These cover Cisco Catalyst, Aruba CX and AOS-S, HPE, FortiGate, Juniper EX, SonicWall, Palo Alto and Sophos models. Ports sit in their real groups and order: on an Aruba CX 6100, ports 49-52 are at the left, followed by 1-48 in blocks of 12.
- **Real port numbers:** uplinks are numbered as on the device (49-52 rather than 1-4), and named ports keep their names (HA, MGMT, X1-X4).
- **Real shapes:** RJ45 jacks are drawn taller than wide with their latch notch; SFP and QSFP ports are drawn as wider, darker cages, in proportion to the real connectors.
- **More models:** devices with no layout on file use a built-in catalog, now with uplink side and numbering.

## Documentation pages

- Tabs under the toolbar show letter pages that follow the diagram, built from the open configurations and supplementary data:
  - **Port map:** front panels with ports colored by role and filled when the link is up, and a table of the ports in each role with their VLANs and link details.
  - **Wireless connections:** each access point with its switch port, model, address, speed and link state, plus links below 1 Gbps.
  - **VLANs and routing:** VLANs with their addresses, routed interfaces, routes and default gateways, and any address used on two devices.
  - **Trunk VLANs:** native and tagged VLANs for each group of trunk, access point and uplink ports.
- **Visio** downloads the diagram and every page in one file. **This page only**, an **SVG image of this page** and **Printable pages** (every page) are in the menu next to it.

## A clearer diagram page with more room

- One toolbar: what to draw, the device and levels, **Show** (device types and drawing style), **Devices**, **Stencils**, **Document** and the downloads. The lists open as menus over the diagram instead of pushing it down.
- **Full screen** gives the diagram the whole window; Esc returns.
- The diagram fills the rest of the window.

## Stencils

- **Searchable:** when you choose a stencil for a device, type a model, part number or file name to narrow the list.
- **Access points** are listed for stencils too.
- **Front views:** automatic matching prefers a device's front view over its rear view.
- **Correct generation:** a stencil for another generation of a series (an FG-120F for a FortiGate-120G) is no longer chosen automatically, because its ports are in other places. You can still choose it yourself.
