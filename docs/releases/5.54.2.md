# Config Inspector 5.54.2

## Fix

- **Port modules on stencil devices:** devices drawn with a vendor stencil or a NetworkDiagramStudio hardware shape now show the module (optic, DAC or AOC cable end, RJ45 module) inside each cage in use, sized to the stencil's ports, as on drawn faceplates. Before, only the legend listed them.
- **Module shapes from NetworkDiagramStudio packs:** when a pack chooses module shapes (switch or firewall SR optic, LR optic, DAC cable end), those pictures fill the cages and the legend, and they come through in the Visio export.
