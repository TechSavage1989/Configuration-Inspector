# Config Inspector 5.28.1

## Site overview tabs

- **Site overview** now has a row of tabs at the top, like Supplementary data: **Settings**, **VLANs**, **Duplicate addresses**, **Shared subnets** and **Device mentions**, each with its count. One section is shown at a time.
- The page scrolls as one instead of having scroll areas inside each table. The tab row, section title and column headings stay at the top while you scroll.
