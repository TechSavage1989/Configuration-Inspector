# Config Inspector 5.60.0

## Stacks as close as they sit in the rack

- **One device per stack:** a stack is now drawn as one device with its members one grid step apart (9 px), instead of separate rows with channels between them. The stack is named below its last member ("VSF stack · 5 members").
- **Member labels in the member:** each member's number and model are inside its colored block at the left ("Member 4", "JL256A"), next to its ports, so nothing sits over the port area.
- **Stack cables stay with the stack:** VSF cables between neighbouring members run straight across the gap. A ring's closing cable, and cables between ports in the same row, run out through the gap and along the side of the stack instead of looping around the page.
- **Links between the ports:** a link from a lower member runs up between the ports of the members above it, never across a port, the member block or a module title. Chassis leaders follow the same rule.

## Links take the short way

- **No loops into the page header:** a link that leaves a device downward no longer climbs over the top row of devices when there is room below.
- **Fewer bends:** links run in neighbouring tracks next to other links instead of taking extra bends to keep their distance. A detour to avoid a crossing is used only when it is at most half as long again and adds at most two bends.
- **Tighter space between rows:** the space between rows is cut to the tracks the links use, and any tighter layout that would add a bend or a detour is not used.
- Names of stacks and chassis are placed between the links where they leave the device.
