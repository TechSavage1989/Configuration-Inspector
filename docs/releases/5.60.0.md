# Config Inspector 5.60.0

## Stacks drawn as they sit in the rack

- **One device per stack:** a stack is now drawn as one device with its members in a column, instead of separate rows with channels between them. The stack is named below its last member ("VSF stack · 5 members").
- **Evenly spaced, as close as the links allow:** the gap between members is as tall as the links that run through the busiest gap need, and every gap in the stack is the same. A stack with no links between members keeps its members one grid step apart.
- **No line across a member:** a link leaves a top-row port upward and a bottom-row port downward into the gap beside it, runs along the gap to the side of the stack, and up or down the side in its own lane. The stack is given the room beside it that these lanes and their port labels need.
- **Member labels in the member:** each member's number and model are inside its colored block at the left ("Member 4", "JL256A"), next to its ports.
- **Stack cables stay with the stack:** VSF cables between neighbouring members run straight across the gap. A ring's closing cable, and cables between ports in the same row, run along the side of the stack that has fewer links instead of looping around the page.
- **Chassis:** a link to an inner module runs between the ports, never across a port or a module title.

## Links take the short way

- **No loops into the page header:** a link that leaves a device downward no longer climbs over the top row of devices when there is room below.
- **Fewer bends:** links run in neighbouring tracks next to other links instead of taking extra bends to keep their distance. A detour to avoid a crossing is used only when it is at most half as long again and adds at most two bends.
- **Tighter space between rows:** the space between rows is cut to the tracks the links use, and any tighter layout that would add a bend or a detour is not used.
- **No lines on top of one another:** two links never run along the same stretch. Where links leave neighbouring ports on a chassis module, each takes its own lane or its own track, so every line can be followed.
- Names of stacks and chassis are placed between the links where they leave the device. A chassis model line with no room between its links is shown beside the chassis name instead of being cut short.
