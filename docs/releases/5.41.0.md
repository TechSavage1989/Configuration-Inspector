# Config Inspector 5.41.0

## Link types by cable and speed

- Links are named and colored by their cable or fiber type and speed, as in a typical site drawing: "10 Gbps SR fiber" (red), "10 Gbps LR fiber" (blue), "10 Gbps SFP+ DAC" (black), "1 Gbps copper" and "Local handoff" (yellow), "AP copper" (green). The media comes from the port's reported media (`show interfaces status`), or else from its connector (SFP cage or RJ45).
- **Editable colors and names:** double-click a link type in the legend, or right-click a link while editing, to change its color and name. Every link of that type and its legend entry change together (kept on this computer for all diagrams).

## Diagram editing

- **Wireless connections** pages can be edited like the diagram (move access points, change labels, reroute links, undo and redo), each with its own edits.
- **Labels on several lines:** double-click a label to edit it in a dialog; Enter starts a new line, Ctrl+Enter applies.
- **Text always fits:** a box grows to fit a longer or taller label, away from where its link meets it.
- **Compact layout:** access points and other endpoints sit above and below their switch, within its width where they fit, instead of running off to one side.
- **Fewer crossings:** a link that crosses another now takes any route that avoids the crossing, however long.

## Stencil port points

- **Better automatic port points:** when a stencil's connection points are not exactly the device's ports (rack-ear points, a missing or merged point), the device's port groups are matched to the stencil's columns of points in order, each group starting at a block of jacks, so ports land on their own points without hand work.
- **Port points editor:** opens on the whole device with no scrollbar; scroll to zoom at the pointer, drag with the middle button or hold Space to pan, and **Fit** to see it all again.
