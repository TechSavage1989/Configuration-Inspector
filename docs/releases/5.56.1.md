# Config Inspector 5.56.1

## Fixes

- **Stacks of different sizes:** on the Core network page, every stack keeps its own column. Before, when a stack with more members sat to the right of a shorter one, its extra members were drawn under the shorter stack and its frame stretched across both.
- **Tighter stacks:** stack members sit close together. A link that drops straight from one member to the next no longer reserves a routing track, and the label band on that side is one text line high, so the gap between members is about a third of what it was. Other links keep the room they need.
