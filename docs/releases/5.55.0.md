# Config Inspector 5.55.0

## Link editing like NetworkDiagramStudio

- **Select a link** in the diagram editor to see its bends as squares, its port ends as fixed dots, and a **+** on each longer middle stretch that adds bends.
- **Bends:** drag a square to move a bend, or click it to select it and press **Delete** to remove it (the stretches beside it straighten) or the arrow keys to nudge it (Shift for bigger steps, Alt for one pixel).
- **Snapping:** bends and stretches snap to other links' corners and to device edges and centers, with a dashed guide where they snap. Hold **Alt** while dragging to turn snapping off.
- **Link tools:** the edit bar's **Link** row names the selection and offers **Remove point**, **Edit bend points** (every point as numbers, with Remove and Add bends) and **Reset route**. Right-click a link for **Edit bend points** too. Port ends always stay attached.

## Paper size

- **Any page size:** choose Letter, Legal, Tabloid (11 x 17), ANSI C (17 x 22), ANSI D (22 x 34), A4, A3 or A2, in automatic, landscape or portrait orientation, under **Document → Paper**. The choice is remembered.
- **Automatic scaling:** each diagram is arranged for the chosen sheet and scales to fill it. On larger sheets, devices and text print bigger, up to twice their size. A diagram too small to read on the sheet still continues on more pages.
- Documentation pages (port maps, wireless connections, VLANs and routing, trunk VLANs) fill the chosen sheet, and Visio, SVG and printable downloads use it.
